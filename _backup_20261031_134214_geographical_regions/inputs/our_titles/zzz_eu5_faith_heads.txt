# The heads of the Christian faiths the conversion keeps: the Pope, and the four patriarchs EU5
# seats at Constantinople, Alexandria, Sis and Mosul. Written by _fix_politics_output.py.
#
# The keys are vanilla's own (k_papal_state, k_orthodox, d_coptic_papacy, d_apostolic_church,
# d_patriarchate_in_the_east) because a vanilla faith names its head of rite by key — a faith whose
# head title nobody declares has no head at all. Each is declared in vanilla's head-of-faith shape:
# landless, so the seat is a de jure pointer and nobody's ground, and with the heir fields the
# engine reads to keep the office filled across a succession.
#
# The same keys used to be landless shims in zz_vanilla_titulars.txt, and the titular guard strips
# a shim the moment it is held — which for a head of faith is the first day of the game. So the
# shims and their guard entries were removed, and the real declarations moved here.
